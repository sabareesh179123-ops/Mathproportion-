1. Direct Proportion Formula ↑ ↑  /  ↓ ↓
x₁ / y₁ = x₂ / y₂   or   x = k · y

Rule: As x increases, y increases at the same constant ratio k = x / y.

Used in: Q1 (iii, v, vi), Question 2 (Pencils), Question 4 (Sleep Charts)
2. Inverse Proportion Formula ↑ ↓  /  ↓ ↑
x₁ × y₁ = x₂ × y₂   or   x · y = k

Rule: As x increases, y decreases so that their product k = x × y stays constant.

Used in: Q1 (i, ii, iv), Question 3 (Building Water Tank Supply)

2. If 24 pencils cost ₹120, how much will 20 such pencils cost?

Formula Used (Direct Proportion) x₁ / y₁ = x₂ / y₂
Step 1: Identify Proportion

Buying more pencils increases the total cost proportionally → Direct Proportion.

Step 2: Set Known & Unknown Values

x₁ = 24 pencils,  y₁ = ₹120   |   x₂ = 20 pencils,  y₂ = ?

Step 3: Apply Direct Proportion Formula

24 / 120 = 20 / y₂  ⇒  y₂ = (20 × 120) / 24

Step 4: Calculate Unit Cost & Final Answer

Unit cost = ₹120 ÷ 24 = ₹5 per pencil  →  20 × ₹5 = ₹100

3. A tank on a building has enough water to supply 20 families for 6 days. If 10 more families move in there, how long will the water last? What assumptions do you need to make to work out this problem?

Formula Used (Inverse Proportion) x₁ × y₁ = x₂ × y₂
Step 1: Find Total Families

Initial = 20 families. 10 more move in → x₂ = 20 + 10 = 30 families.

Step 2: Identify Proportion

More families sharing a fixed water tank = fewer days → Inverse Proportion.

Step 3: Apply Formula

20 × 6 = 30 × y₂  ⇒  120 = 30 · y₂

Step 4: Solve for Days (y₂)

y₂ = 120 / 30 = 4 days

💬 Math Talk — Assumptions Needed:
  1. Every family consumes the same average amount of water per day.
  2. No extra water is added or refilled into the tank during this period.
  3. No water is lost to tank leaks or wastage.
